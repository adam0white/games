Crumbwar · Classic

    ClassicFeastRush

    Four ant colonies share one field. Carry crumbs home to score for your colony. Bite enemy ants from behind: an ant bites what is in front of it, and a carrier cannot bite at all. Your own nest is safe. An enemy nest hurts.

    Drag to steer on a phone, or point with the mouse. Hold Sprint, Space or the mouse button to run. Ants marked bot are computer players that fill the room.
