# Story reference

This is the continuity reference for authored fiction, not a universal text store. Dialogue stays next to the behavior that presents it. All characters are fictional; no biography describes the owner's family.

- [Broad story](overview.md): the direction and open questions.
- [Characters](characters.md): voices, desires, relationships and obligations.
- [History](history.md): established events and object provenance.
- [Playable episode](episode.md): disclosure order and future threads.

When adding a fact, check these pages and record it in the appropriate place. A planned event is not an event that has happened. A fact known to its speaker is not automatically known to the player or every HF person. Story options do not establish repair competence, a customer contract or apprentice learning.

The finite object catalogue lives in `src/game/catalogue.ts`: eight neighbour/household requests alongside the two opening lamps, with a separate paper-street project waiting for a compatible gear. Runtime text stays beside authored object data; this index records relationships and history rather than duplicating every line.

The optional post-week sample case and separate household light are authored in `src/game/neighbourhood.ts`. They preserve their own acceptance, parts and return histories rather than becoming repeating catalogue stock.

The selected working-life episodes are authored in `src/game/operations.ts`: one collection afternoon, a later month and the following year. Their transport objects, supplied parts, loans and arrangements are finite. `src/game/personal-project.ts` owns reversible alterations and one later occasion for the existing paper street. Neither module establishes an economy or completes unplayed repairs.
