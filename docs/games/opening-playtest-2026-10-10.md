## Interpretation for the next playable iteration

Preserve the one-action curious beginning. Let the household emerge through particular objects and optional conversation: Mum’s reading habits, a room that has changed since the player left, Dad’s unfinished projects, and friends’ small routines. The setting is fictional; its people do not represent the owner’s relatives.

Optional lines are conversation, not repair practice or a relationship currency. They can be skipped without closing a route. Ordinary warmth can coexist with practical obligations: a friend can be interested and still have to walk the dog. An invitation to observe is a narrative beginning, not evidence of learned repair behavior.

Object differences should change what the player sees and does, beyond a new name attached to the same repair. Results need to appear near the player’s attention. Checking takes simulated time; a break can have a practical consequence while remaining an ordinary part of the afternoon.

The next owner playtest remains the acceptance dependency for this iteration’s feel. The independent-apprentice acceptance checks remain separate in [the MVP target](../mvp.md#acceptance).
