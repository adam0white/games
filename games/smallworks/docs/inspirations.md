# Design references

Researched 2026-10-09 using developer/publisher sources. These establish described mechanics, not proof that a mechanic caused commercial success. Proposed lessons below are our interpretation; no games were played during this research pass.

| Reference and primary source | Described approach | Proposed lesson here | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Assemble with Care — ustwo games](https://ustwogames.co.uk/our-games/assemble-with-care/) | Restoring objects is connected to their owners and personal stories; nostalgic objects and handcrafted presentation | Make the first object interesting and personally meaningful before presenting business controls | Its authored story does not establish our procedural repairs or learned delegation |
| [Mechanical Pencil — retractable pen breakdown](https://mechanical-pencil.com/products/pen) | Interactive teardown and part exploration, with exploded illustrations, 3D/2D views and an animated mechanism | Let inspection reveal the actual small parts and causal relationships rather than a generic object icon | Owner direction recorded 2026-10-10. Its authored illustrations are not our assets; use original models and a bounded fictional repair model. Full 3D interaction is a visual direction, not evidence of physical repair realism. |
| [Autonauts — Denki](https://denki.itch.io/autonauts) | Teach workerbots by doing an action, then repeat and run their work | Teaching should be visible and the benefit of relinquishing work immediate | HF people need conditional understanding, agency and exceptions; recorded loops alone are insufficient |
| [Football Manager 26 — responsibilities and delegation](https://www.footballmanager.com/the-dugout/delegating-success-football-manager-26) | Reassign responsibilities to staff; relevant attributes and available staff affect delegation | Let players choose their involvement and develop people capable of carrying a role | Avoid importing its full management interface into the home opening |
| [Hardspace: Shipbreaker — Blackbird Interactive](https://www.blackbirdinteractive.com/shipbreaker) | Hands-on salvage earns credits for equipment and larger contracts | Material recovery and learning to handle an object can support progression | Do not assume detailed 3D cutting, hazards or a debt campaign are necessary for our reuse mechanic |

## Our unproven proposition

The distinctive ambition is a sequence of changing roles and game feel, with continuity: home tinkerer → teacher → manager → owner of businesses, while returning to the bench remains worthwhile. These sources offer useful pieces. They do not prove that this combination is a new genre, that it will sell, or that its first prestige will feel satisfying.

The proposed MVP tests the first transition. Later genre changes should be earned through playtests, not assumed from this research.
