Ant Promotion: Nemesis

Eat. Grow. Get promoted. Get even.

An experiment. The animal that kills you becomes your rival. It gets a name, talks to you, remembers you and gets stronger each time it wins. Grow big enough and eat it. Play the main game

Your ant

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to walk. On a keyboard use the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Every animal has a power number over its head. So do you.
  • Green number: weaker than you. Touch it to eat it.
  • Red number: stronger than you. Touch it and you die.
  • The pale wedge is what an animal can see. Walk into it and the animal notices you: !
  • » means it is faster than you. A fast strong animal that notices you will catch you. A fast weak one will get away, so sneak up from behind.
  • Fill the bar to get promoted. You pick 1 of 3 new animals each time.
  • If you die you go back one animal, or you can start again as an ant.
  • Your rival. The first animal that kills you becomes your rival. It gets a name and a coloured ring, and it talks in speech bubbles. When it is off the screen, an arrow in its colour shows where it is.
  • Your rival does not go away. It stays in the world, comes looking for you now and then, and each time it kills you it gets one level stronger and gets a scar.
  • Rivals act differently. Some wait still and see far. Some follow you and strike while you are eating. Some charge and chase for a long time. Some are bullies that run once you are nearly as strong.
  • When your number is bigger than your rival's, eat it for a big bonus. It goes on your list of rivals beaten, and the next animal that kills you becomes your new rival.
  • The rival's name and words are sometimes written by a computer language model. Without a connection the game uses its own list.
