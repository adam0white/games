## Preserved future ideas

- **A changing genre:** cozy home tinkering → business management → idle conglomerate. Each transition changes the player's decisions and presentation.
- **Return to cozy:** maintain a personal bench and meaningful personal repairs at any scale. Leaving management temporarily should not collapse a competently delegated business.
- **Origin matters:** early training, recovered parts, promises, relationships and workshop culture continue to affect later outcomes; keep specific histories as well as aggregates.
- **Objects with histories:** sentimental value, wear, previous repairs and provenance; expensive does not always mean meaningful.
- **More interesting object families:** music boxes, mechanical toys and other familiar objects, selected for substantial reuse of existing actions and parts before adding bespoke repair systems.
- **Hard projects:** put aside an object, return it honestly, obtain a missing part, learn a method and revisit it. An unfinished repair is not automatically a failure.
- **Reuse and recycling:** compatibility and condition, donor objects, recoverable parts, material recovery; useful choices without cataloging every screw.
- **Adaptation rather than a fixed tech tree:** encountered difficulties make new methods or organizational changes relevant. Start from authored methods before considering open-ended generation.
- **People rather than interchangeable workers:** different aptitudes, commitments, ambitions, teaching preferences, trust and willingness; choosing a successor can matter beyond throughput.
- **Faith and inner life:** natural, consequential representation; broader traditions and spiritual development belong to sourced HF decisions.
- **Football-manager-style business:** hire, develop, assign responsibility, evaluate imperfect reports, and choose how involved to remain.
- **Offline progress:** desirable idle convenience, deferred until deterministic stepping, bounded catch-up and save integrity are measured. No unbounded wall-clock rewards in the first MVP.
- **Beyond this theme:** repeat the progression in other domains only after one complete example works.
- **Non-game apprentices:** learn repeated user practices and offer bounded, inspectable delegation on demand. Separate future project; real-world action needs its own authority and controls.

## Object inspection in 3D, owner direction 2026-10-10

Target: after the finite school campaign and first workshop handoff are playable, replace or extend flat object inspection with original small 3D objects. Rotate an object, see accessible joins and wear, and deliberately inspect a relevant surface. Start with the lamp, torch and one exposed mechanical assembly before expanding the catalogue. Use original procedural geometry or commissioned assets; reference games inform interaction pacing, not copied art or characters.

Keep visible evidence bounded by observation. An uninspected model must not disclose a hidden fault through a glowing part or fault-specific cutaway. View movement alone does not grant diagnosis or an HF method. Inspection actions, tests, repairs and results remain deterministic and replayable; record camera state only if it changes which evidence the player can acquire. Reduced-motion, touch controls and a clear text equivalent remain available. Full artwork production, unconstrained disassembly and physically simulated every-part repairs remain later decisions.

## Findings from the extended playthrough, 2026-10-10

- A self-owned item returned unchecked must remain physically recoverable; completing the real check, not waiting or retrieval alone, earns progress. This is implemented and independently reviewed in the current continuity pass.
- Dad's optional cabinet work needs an actual proposed appointment rather than opaque repeated requests. The bounded current pass uses existing HF decisions and promises; a general calendar/planner remains outside scope.
- A new school holiday should visibly begin a fresh morning and preserve actual elapsed actor time, rather than retaining the prior afternoon's minute. This is implemented and independently reviewed in the current pass.
- **Review by 2026-11-09:** show the house accumulating its actual checked repairs. Keep ownership, unfinished work and absent collections honest; do not fabricate a gallery of universally finished objects.
- **Review by 2026-11-09:** more observed comparisons for mechanical families where a real alternative hypothesis exists. Preserve the small, clear early repair; avoid adding implausible failure options solely to disguise a straightforward mechanism.
- **Review by 2026-11-09:** make repeated family time less generic through run-dependent ordinary conversation and actually received memories. Tea should not become a required testing currency, a relationship purchase or an invented physiology subsystem.
