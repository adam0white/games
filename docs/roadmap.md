# Broad roadmap

2026-10-09. Only planning is authorized. M0 is the current delivery; M1–M4 are the proposed standalone MVP. Later stages preserve ambition without scheduling implementation.

## Near milestones

| Milestone | Player experience | Capability / question | Stop when |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0: establish the project | A concrete proposal to review | Can the opening and first handoff support the larger ambition? | Local repository, owner directions, researched references, reviewed roadmap and MVP draft exist. Adam reviews the MVP before code starts. |
| M1: family tinkering | Fix one meaningful object at home; discover a second object you cannot yet finish | Is repair itself interesting, with informative tests and understandable consequences? | One short playable episode supports repair, putting work aside and later resumption. Adam enjoys the core decisions without hiring. |
| M2: friends in the garage | Handle a small queue, recover useful parts, keep or renegotiate a promise | Can parts, partial work and customer relationships make a small business feel real? | A compact garage episode has understandable quality, time and cost tradeoffs, and persistent repairs. No simulated market is needed. |
| M3: teach one apprentice | Demonstrate, supervise and hand over a complete repair | Does teaching change a person's decisions on unfamiliar variants? | A generic HF control distinguishes learned procedure from skill-only growth and click replay; the game visibly demonstrates it. Framework scope is selected only after checking released HF. |
| M4: first prestige | The apprentice owns an entire repair while you choose broader work or return to your bench | Does reduced supervision create enjoyable freedom with continuing consequences? | Independent completion on both supported held-out variants, including verification and customer outcomes; appropriate escalation on an out-of-scope variant. Play a two-job post-handoff episode while arranging work or returning to the personal repair. Adam accepts the standalone game. |

Do not begin M2 until M1 is enjoyable. Do not complete every possible HF faculty before testing M3. If the opening is dull or delegation is incomprehensible, improve that milestone rather than introducing another business tier.

## Longer-term stages

| Stage | Change in feel and responsibility | Earlier work that remains useful | Activation condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workshop | Scheduling, recruiting, training, quality and promises | Apprentices, methods, tools, parts and customer relationships | MVP accepted; a small crew demonstrably adds decisions rather than chores |
| Repair service business | Contracts, pickup/delivery, specialists, a bench lead | Reputation earned from actual repairs; trained staff handle familiar work | One manager can carry a bounded operation without player rescue |
| Multiple operations | Managers and supply relationships across repair, refurbishment, salvage or logistics | Recovered parts, expertise, relationships and transferable methods | A second operation has a distinct useful role; simulation cost is measured |
| Idle conglomerate | Allocate responsibility and capital; intervene in exceptional cases; revisit a bench | Founding crew, inherited practices, remembered objects, business history | Management is fun at a small scale; a documented simulation strategy preserves meaningful consequences |
| Simulated economy | Endogenous prices, supply, demand, competition and interdependence | Firms built from actual capabilities, not only purchased multipliers | Single-player businesses are robust; economy gets its own bounded goal and controls |
| Multiplayer / MMORPG | Persistent organizations, other players, markets and cooperation | Continuity of workers, organizations and local craftsmanship | Clear player demand and a separate architecture/security/operations decision |

No implementation date is assigned to these stages. Review the deferred ambitions at MVP acceptance, or on 2026-11-09 if the MVP has not reached acceptance. This is a review date, not an automation.

## Preserved future ideas

- **A changing genre:** cozy home tinkering → business management → idle conglomerate. Each transition changes the player's decisions and presentation.
- **Return to cozy:** maintain a personal bench and meaningful personal repairs at any scale. Leaving management temporarily should not collapse a competently delegated business.
- **Origin matters:** early training, recovered parts, promises, relationships and workshop culture continue to affect later outcomes; keep specific histories as well as aggregates.
- **Objects with histories:** sentimental value, wear, previous repairs and provenance; expensive does not always mean meaningful.
- **More interesting object families:** music boxes, mechanical toys and other familiar objects, selected for substantial reuse of existing actions and parts before adding bespoke repair systems.
- **Hard projects:** put aside an object, return it honestly, obtain a missing part, learn a method and revisit it. An unfinished repair is not automatically a failure.
- **Reuse and recycling:** compatibility and condition, donor objects, recoverable parts, material recovery; useful choices without cataloging every screw.
- **Adaptation rather than a fixed tech tree:** encountered difficulties make new methods or organizational changes relevant. Start from authored methods before considering open-ended generation.
- **People rather than interchangeable workers:** different aptitudes, commitments, ambitions, teaching preferences, trust and willingness; choosing a successor can matter beyond throughput.
- **Faith and inner life:** natural, consequential representation; broader traditions and spiritual development belong to sourced HF decisions.
- **Football-manager-style business:** hire, develop, assign responsibility, evaluate imperfect reports, and choose how involved to remain.
- **Offline progress:** desirable idle convenience, deferred until deterministic stepping, bounded catch-up and save integrity are measured. No unbounded wall-clock rewards in the first MVP.
- **Beyond this theme:** repeat the progression in other domains only after one complete example works.
- **Non-game apprentices:** learn repeated user practices and offer bounded, inspectable delegation on demand. Separate future project; real-world action needs its own authority and controls.

## Unresolved decisions

Public title and repository visibility; eventual license; exact opening object art and input style; how the player character expresses fatigue and learning; elapsed playtime target after a first prototype; longer-run simulation fidelity. Resolve only when the next milestone needs the answer.
