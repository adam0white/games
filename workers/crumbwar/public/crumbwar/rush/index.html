Crumbwar · Rush

    ClassicFeastRush

    A two-minute colony race. Carry crumbs home. Grab the golden crumb for 5. Stand on the hill in the middle to score. Seeds give ↑ speed or a ◇ shield. Finish the goal shown at the top for bonus points. The last 20 seconds pay double.

    Drag to steer on a phone, or point with the mouse. Hold Sprint, Space or the mouse button to run. Ants marked bot are computer players that fill the room.
