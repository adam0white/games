How to play

  • Drag anywhere to walk. On a keyboard use the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Every animal has a power number over its head. So do you.
  • Green number: weaker than you. Touch it to eat it.
  • Red number: stronger than you. Touch it and you die.
  • The pale wedge is what an animal can see. Walk into it and the animal notices you: !
  • » means it is faster than you. A fast strong animal that notices you will catch you. A fast weak one will get away, so sneak up from behind.
  • No food on the screen? A green arrow at the edge points to the nearest animal you can eat. A red arrow points to a stronger animal that has seen you.
  • Fill the bar to get promoted. You pick 1 of 3 new animals each time. Picking fast again makes you faster each time. Picking sneaky again makes you harder to see each time.
  • A hunter gets tired. Keep running: it slows down, then gives up and rests for a few seconds.
  • Animals hunt each other too. A red dashed line shows who is chasing whom. A hunter that is busy is easier to sneak past.
  • Wild rules are the normal game; the Rules button in the menu switches to Classic. Wild adds two things. A strong animal has a thick hide: it lives through one bite, then the hide needs 25 seconds. And every 45 seconds a stronger animal nearby hunts you for 5 seconds, wherever it is looking.
  • Your rival. The animal that kills you becomes your rival. It gets a name, a crown and a coloured ring, and it talks in speech bubbles. When it is off the screen, an arrow in its colour shows where it is.
  • Your rival does not go away. It stays in the world, comes looking for you now and then, and each time it kills you it gets 10 stronger (up to 30) and gets a scar.
  • Rivals act differently. Some wait still and see far. Some follow you and strike while you are eating. Some charge and chase for a long time. Some are bullies that run once you are nearly as strong.
  • When your number is bigger than your rival's, eat it for a big bonus. The next animal that kills you becomes your new rival.
  • If you die you go back one animal, or you can start again as an ant.
