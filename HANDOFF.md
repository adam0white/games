## Current direction

**Small Works** is a diagnostic workshop game. Electrical measurements follow a circuit model; mechanical measurements follow a drive model. Shared symptoms can have different causes. Chosen governor settings must meet a visible requested pace. The comparison meter retains readings across test conditions. Owner-specific use checks distinguish a running bench object from a checked repair.

Stock, cash and one workshop clock make work selection consequential. The personal paper theatre remains available. A community gathering has garden, reading and theatre formats; actual equipment, preparation time, presence and completed activities determine the result. The player can prepare at most two formats.

Sam learns only from actual attended observations through published HF 2.2.1. Successful follow-up evidence supports intervention teaching. HF chooses among offered activities and methods; the host does not grant a correct repair policy. Pending requests, accepted responsibility, ordinary needs and personal commitments now have an explicit lifecycle. Teaching windows, missed observations and renewed consent have focused checks. Source review and play must verify that this is understandable and causally sound.
