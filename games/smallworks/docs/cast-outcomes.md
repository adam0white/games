## Boundaries

Sam’s old report and the new monetary amounts are authored scenario particulars, not recovered real reports or imported financial facts. The registry and existing disclosures supply the conflict; this edition supplies the finite paper account. Original source rows are the canonical-cast table, not player quotations. Nothing here counts as a lab-panel result.

Nora’s self-use trial records one fictional workshop user. It does not establish that the person excluded by her earlier accessibility decision can use the result. That person’s trial needs their own consent and presence. It is no certification or human acceptance. Contact with her brother and family reconciliation require a separate voluntary exchange; neither is required or forced by attribution.

Rafi’s loans settle the recipient accounts and leave real workshop debt outstanding. The game does not promise wages or erase that debt. Repairing or returning the fan does not settle money. Unfinished returns remain unfinished.

Halil’s priced reopening ships. A rent-debt agreement needs the creditor’s actual known terms and separate assent; the workshop cannot author that assent for them. Medical improvement needs an appropriate separately supported clinical scene. Teaching Sam requires actual attended teaching; no biography-derived method is granted. Nuran remains deceased. Smoking, grief and rent arrears remain unresolved. Faith is neither payment nor a score.

The portable cast registry remains a set of possibilities. These separate-input or guardrail-dependent alternatives are not promised additional features in this batch. Feasible engineering in the selected four finite paths is implemented here. Shared shelf ownership, medical transport and other games’ accounts retain their existing admission rules.
