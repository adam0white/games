How to play

  • Drag anywhere to walk. On a keyboard use the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Green number: weaker than you. Touch it to eat it. Red number: stronger. Touch it and you die.
  • The pale wedge is what an animal can see. Walk into it and the animal notices you: ! » means it is faster than you.
  • Fill the bar to get promoted. If you die you go back one animal.
  • The meadow evolves. Every animal has a few traits: speed, how far and how wide it sees, how long it keeps chasing, how early it runs from you, and how much it keeps to a herd. A new animal is bred from the ones that did best against you: hunters that chased you longest or caught you, and prey that got away.
  • Marks show a strong trait: speed lines behind a fast animal, a bright eye ring on one that sees far, red chevrons over one that will not give up, and a dotted ring round a watchful prey animal.
  • Two deaths within a minute and the meadow eases off for a while. Reset the meadow from the menu at any time.
