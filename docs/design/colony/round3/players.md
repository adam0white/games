## Ranked design changes

The clean-room checks below use the exclusions and permitted genre conventions in `02-spec.md:25–52`. The rankings and designs are proposals.

1. **Ship the seed → meal → husk → shelter → reroute sequence first.** It answers both halves of the verdict with one interaction. Shorten the route to improve pace. Let the player choose the location of cover to add depth. Test a husk on each site and require the sheltered route to change with it. **Clean-room check:** founding bloom, movable cover and the burned hillside are Cinderhill’s own premise.
2. **Make the second decision compete with the first.** By minute three, the same worker must have two worthwhile jobs. On a dry hill, gathering more seed pays first. With a low nursery and approaching rain, diverting diggers pays first. Before commissioning more days, let the owner play both hills with equal starting stocks and inspect whether he changes assignments. **Clean-room check:** worker allocation, free digging and terrain-dependent room value fall within §1. Use crews attached to jobs and trails. Keep numbered pheromone groups and adjacency upgrades excluded.
3. **Give every order a visible chain of consequences.** Keep Field and Ember as equal test variants. In both, the selected crew should turn, a carried seed should remain distinguishable, and the other-layer inset should show the meal even if the player stays above ground. Make the first player-fed adult join the assigned route in view. **Clean-room check:** use Cinderhill’s carrier silhouettes, seed, brood and palette. This requires no narrator, borrowed UI or reference-game assets.
4. **Give a short visit an ending and a long visit another problem.** A two-minute player leaves with a saved shelter. A five-minute player has to staff a changing hill. An established colony can retain an 8–12-minute day. In a friends’ session the guest’s first action should alter a route or shelter both players can recognize. **Clean-room check:** persistent terrain, shared bloom and sister nests are compatible with §1. They add no reference mission, species progression or meta-currency.
