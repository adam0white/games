# Triage of the public games, 2026-11-23

The owner asked for a review of every game on `play.adamwhite.work`, because the older games fall below the bake-off prototypes. Each game was played in a phone-profile browser (Playwright WebKit, iPhone 13, 390 by 664) with lab uploads blocked, and its code layout and size were read. Earlier owner verdicts come from the HF repository's `docs/lab/ranking-2026-10-20.md`.

An agent finds defects and structure. It does not vote on fun. "Done" below means acted on; every other row is a proposal for the owner.

## Pieces worth extracting

From the older games:

- **Insist or let go** (The Two Voices): a suggestion shows whether the person would agree, and holding the button insists at a cost in trust. It is the smallest working form of "people can say no".
- **Speech bubbles on stick figures** (The Rope): five answers readable at once over the scene.
- **Dig grid and cross-section** (The Warren): draw on stone, people decide when to dig. A candidate for an Ant Promotion nest.
- **Timed building view** (Smoke on the Stairs): a round clock, waves of hazard and a row of people states.
- **Light and warmth** (The Left Hand): being seen against being cold. A candidate hazard for Lantern.
- **Ink story runner** (His Word, Three Days): one runner for short stories, with a clock that each choice spends.

Already shared through `@adam0white/lab-kit` and not worth moving: the notebook menu, pace control, Skip, tutorial card and recording notice.

From the prototypes, for the shared-code work: touch input, audio unlock, pause, menu, save, shop and the feedback button.
