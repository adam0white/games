# Cinderhill rebuild review — cold builder

Checked — Both the spec and the build produced the reported result. The spec delayed growth and pressure, and the build omitted the opening’s central choice.  
Checked — The owner’s colony sessions produced no births or threats. The first delivery took 73 seconds in one session and never arrived in several others.  
Checked — The second review added founding bloom to M1, but the build order and bot acceptance still let a ten-minute quota stand in for the opening.  
Proposal — Make a complete first decision and its consequence the first playable milestone. Test route, labor and terrain tradeoffs before expanding the campaign.  
Proposal — Give the builder one rules source, three authored day fixtures, and explicit acceptance for each milestone before implementation begins.

## Before step one

Ratify the opening clock and exit rule, seed and planting ledger, labor ownership, and the two Day 3 positions. Give each authored fixture a map, starting state, expected events and observable pass/fail outcomes. Make `02-spec.md` and its rules tables authoritative, `03-features.md` the acceptance matrix, and `04-roadmap.md` the dependency order. Record measured implementation decisions in `05-tech.md`. Treat the research and toys as evidence. I used §M1 of the roadmap as the build plan because I found no separate rebuild plan in the specified repo paths.

## First ten build steps

1. Capture the legacy opening and its slow or missing delivery as a failing acceptance fixture. Check expected shortest routes with an independent solver.
2. Build the default campaign entry, portrait controls and command previews on the authored opening map. Check handoff from stroke to pinch.
3. Connect the complete seed delivery, larva feeding, husk placement, dusk and next-dawn shelter demonstration. Put this build in front of the owner.
4. Add deterministic worker allocation and order inspection. Demonstrate rerouting a carrier, a blocked crew, and Recall while an alarm is active.
5. Implement one nursery, granary and mound with conserved cargo, usable capacities and visibly different outcomes from different placements.
6. Add real checkpoints and replay before weather or more days. Exercise interrupted writes and restart at the dusk boundary.
7. Add flooding and the traffic-triggered spider. Verify the no-action, Recall and bypass outcomes separately.
8. Play the paired Day 3 positions. Require the better plan to reverse. Revise the rules if both hills reward the same commitment.
9. Finish the six authored Spring days. Run cold-start testing, same-version replay and real-device frame and touch checks on the integrated build.
10. Play the accepted Spring loop in a deployed two-seat room. Verify cold join and recovery before adding the remaining campaign content and versus modes at their gates.
