### What to measure first

1. **Founding on fresh phones.** Check the proposed timeline against command and state timestamps at the actual solo tick rate. Use an existing long-route fixture as a negative control. Ask the player to explain the second husk and identify a route they would change. A faster simulation alone should still fail this gate if the choice has no consequence.
2. **A representative late-game day on iPhone 13 and Pixel 7 class hardware.** Build one repeatable fixture with a dug nest, rival traffic through an entrance, mixed combat, active rain, bloom, many visible castes and a checkpoint during play. Run it at 2,000 ants and stress it at 5,000. Record per-system tick p95/p99/max, tick debt and dropped solo time, frame p95, touch-to-visible feedback, memory, and thermal degradation over a full day. Require 30 fps at 2,000 ants with a frame p95 at or below 33.3 ms, and preserve the 0.5-second order-feedback gate. Add a topology-change burst and context loss to the fixture.
