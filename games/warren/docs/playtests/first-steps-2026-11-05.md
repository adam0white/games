A separate synthetic first-time player used a fresh desktop browser and took actions without reading the game source or documentation. More than one minute elapsed, including an automation retry; the timing was not instrumented precisely. This is not a human playtest.

The player chose chalk and first drew marks that were not connected to the cellar. The player marked beds in the existing cellar and saw the capacity change: “That was a concrete result; I now know the shelter has capacity, but I cannot tell from this alone when the attack will come or if everyone will reach it.” They understood the refuge goal and that riders can kill people outside. They did not see an attack, death, completed excavation or a full outcome. No hard blocker was observed in this single synthetic run.
