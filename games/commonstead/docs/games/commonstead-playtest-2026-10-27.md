# Commonstead playtest, same-tester retest, 2026-10-27

- **Build tested:** live https://commonstead.adamwhite.work at `fe433b8` (`/release.json` `dirty:false`), opened with `?labRunSource=agent`.
- **Previous round:** hf3-commonstead-2026-10-25 (R1; evidence in human-framework `docs/lab/reviews/hf3-commonstead-2026-10-25/`).
- **Testers:** the same 14 lab-panel personas as R1 (Maya, Emre, Nia, Rosa, Jonas, Aisha, Diego, Mei, Samir, Tessa, Pavel, Leila, Alina, Julio). Each was a Claude Sonnet persona agent that had read only its own R1 report.
- **Method:** the [playtest harness](https://github.com/adam0white/human-framework/blob/main/docs/lab/playtest-harness.md) lab driver (`scripts/lab-play.ts`). Each tester played two fresh sessions, a 360x690 touch phone and a 1280x800 desktop, in light mode. Floor 0 and a 20-minute ceiling, with a free stop. Lab Recording stayed on and runs are labelled as agents. No tester submitted feedback text.
- **This is a fictional lab panel.** It is not evidence about recruited players or a population.
- **Caveats:**
  - Several testers misstated their own elapsed minutes. The ledger uses the driver's times.
  - Julio and Tessa chose the optional longer day or the personal-replies day on the title page for one session. Everyone else played the default day.

## Verdict: needs a fix cycle

A **new blocking ask** is an ask with no R1 F-number that is tied to a quit or a conditional ("mixed") return sentence. The R1 synthesis has no item about the storehouse, the meals, the tiredness, the resident closing lines or the commander. Those features arrived after R1, so asks about them are new.

Dispositions: 1 keep, 10 mixed, 3 quit.

### New blocking asks (fix cycle)

1. **The reply day locks the replies behind a walk to "the commander".** This is the asked-for personal-replies mode.
   - Tessa: "both were locked until I walk to the commander at the hub. I do not know who the commander is or how to walk there. Then the replies disappeared even with Nora selected."
   - Leila: "both Say this buttons are disabled with: walk to the workshop hub to talk with the commander. I do not know who the commander is or how I walk."
   - Nia: "Walk the resident to the hub for me when I choose a reply, or say plainly that the resident walks."
   - **Proposed fix:** let the selected resident answer in place, or walk the resident automatically. Keep the reply panel open while that resident stays selected.
2. **The resident closing lines are identical.** Every tester who finished said so.
   - Samir: "All eight still said the same sentence on the win, and two groups of lines on the loss."
   - Diego: "All eight said the same sentence in both sessions."
   - Mei: "Seven of eight said nearly the same sentence."
   - **Proposed fix:** compose each closing line from that resident's own day: their story, the job they did, their tiredness and their shelter.
3. **The disabled Storehouse gives no reason.**
   - Samir: "On desktop it stayed greyed out for about four minutes with no explanation."
   - Diego: "say which prerequisite is missing when Storehouse is disabled (for example "boards 158 of 180")."
   - Julio (phone): "the Storehouse button stays greyed out. I do not know how to proceed on the phone."
   - **Proposed fix:** put the missing prerequisite on or under the button.
4. **The phone map is a thin strip outside placement, and the resident panel is a few lines tall.**
   - Mei: "The window is a few lines tall between the deadline text and the buttons."
   - Pavel: "the "Everyone now" panel opens into a strip two lines high."
   - Alina: "still a thin strip under the speed buttons whenever I am not placing something."
   - **Proposed fix:** at 360 px wide, give the map and the panel fixed minimum heights and let the work buttons scroll instead.
   - Placement readability is an R1 item, partly met. The panel height is new.
5. **Eat and drink spends the storm meals without a warning, and nothing says what cures tiredness.**
   - Samir: "Warn that Eat and drink spends the meals the storm needs, and that very tired people work much more slowly. That cost me the phone run."
   - Alina: "Eating cost 16 meals and nobody looked less tired."
   - Pavel: "Let me rest or shelter a whole group in one order."
   - **Proposed fix:** before spending, state the cost against the storm requirement. Say on screen that rest under a roof cures tiredness. Add a rest-all order.
6. **"Return carried materials first" blocks a story the player cannot clear.**
   - Nia: "Stop blocking a story with "return carried materials first" when I cannot do that."
   - **Proposed fix:** let the resident drop the load automatically, or show a one-tap "drop at hub".

### Still open from R1, or smaller

- Julio, on the longer day: "The disputes, sleeping and idle crews need a visible cause and a clear fix." Proposed fix: name each route dispute and its cause in the work log.
- Rosa and Alina found the intro typo "5 meals per person At 10:00".
- Rosa: the end tally says 2 completed buildings when one was built.
- Rosa: help opens with its top cut off.
- Several testers asked for a second or varied day after a win: Emre, Jonas, Mei, Aisha. This is a content ask, not blocking.
